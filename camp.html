The Mission

Why This Camp Exists

Every dollar raised goes straight back into Socratic Schematics, FRC Team 8885 — covering parts, competition fees, and travel so every student on the team can compete, regardless of circumstance.

Along the way, campers get a genuine, ground-floor introduction to engineering — taught by the same high schoolers who build competition robots year-round.

The Final Project

Each team designs, builds, and programs its own robot car from scratch — then puts it to the test in a friendly competition on the final day, in front of family and friends.

What They'll Learn

Real Skills, Kid-Sized Steps

Electronics

Wire real circuits by hand — batteries, resistors, LEDs, buttons, potentiometers, and small DC motors — and learn how each part actually works.

Coding

Get a real introduction to robotics coding on an Arduino Uno microcontroller using PictoBlox drag-and-drop blocks — inputs, outputs, and logic.

Design & Build

Plan, build, and iterate on a real chassis using LEGO-based parts and motors — as a team, with a builder, wirer, and coder role for every kid.

Ten Days, One Robot

The Curriculum

Every day builds on the last — from a single LED to a fully autonomous robot car, competing on the final showcase day.

Week One — Foundations
1

Circuits & Components

Meet in age-balanced teams and learn how batteries, resistors, LEDs, buttons, potentiometers, and DC motors work — by wiring each one up themselves.

2

Meet the Microcontroller

Wire up an Arduino Uno and power supply, then use block coding to blink, pattern, and control LEDs and a servo motor.

3

Inputs & Logic

Read buttons and potentiometers into the Arduino Uno, print values to a serial monitor, and write if/then logic to react to them.

4

Build the Car

Teams split into builder, wirer, and coder roles to assemble their own robot car's chassis, wiring, and starter code.

Week Two — Compete & Showcase
5

Sumo Challenge

Teams put their car to the test in a friendly sumo-style head-to-head.

6

Maze & Distance Sensing

Add a distance sensor and navigate a maze course.

7

Plow & Push Challenge

Bolt a plow attachment onto the car and learn torque vs. speed by gearing up to push objects out of a ring.

8

Robot World Cup

Combine this week's distance sensing and gearing with a servo-powered kicker for a robot soccer tournament, plus a bonus goalkeeper challenge.

9

Customize & Scrimmage

Morning: teams add a personal touch to their robot. Afternoon: a round-robin scrimmage across every event as a dress rehearsal for showcase day.

10

Final Showcase

Parents and family are invited to watch every team demo the robot they built, wired, and coded themselves.
