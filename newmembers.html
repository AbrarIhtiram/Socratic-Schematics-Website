Who We Are

We're Socratic Schematics

We're Maeser Prep's FIRST Robotics Competition team. Every January FIRST reveals a brand-new game, and in a few short weeks we design, build, wire, and program a robot from scratch to play it against teams from all over.

Plenty of us walked in on day one without knowing what a drivetrain was. We teach each other, try ideas, break things, fix them, and somehow end up laughing through most of it.
